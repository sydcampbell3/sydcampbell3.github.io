About the Data

I built harmonized national and Hawaiʻi utility panels from U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) survey data, reconciling each form's shifting file formats into a consistent series:

  • EIA-861 / 861M (formerly EIA-826): monthly retail sales, revenue, and customers (prices), 1990–2026*
  • EIA-861: annual utility sales, ownership, and operations, 1990–2025
  • EIA-861 reliability: SAIDI, SAIFI, and CAIDI, 2013–2025
  • EIA-923 / 906-920: monthly plant generation and fuel use, 2001–2025
  • EIA-759: pre-923 generation (Hawaiʻi), 1990–2000
  • EIA-860: utility and generator characteristics, 2001–2025

These are supplemented with Hawaiʻi-specific sources:

  • Hawaiʻi PUC annual financial reports, 1990–2025
  • SEC 10-K filings: independent power producer purchases, 2004–2025
  • Rooftop solar panel: distributed PV by utility, 2011–2024

*2026 covers January–July.
